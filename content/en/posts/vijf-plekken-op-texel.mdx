Texel has no shortage of beautiful places, but some of them stay with me. These are five of my favourites — and when they are at their most beautiful.

## De Slufter

Wide, wild and never the same, thanks to the light and the wind. Early in the morning or just before sunset De Slufter is at its most beautiful — and at its quietest.

## Beach pavilion at sunset

For those who love a bit of conviviality without giving up the view. A ceremony with your feet in the sand and a drink the moment you have said yes.

## De Cocksdorp lighthouse

For the couple who like a story with some weight to it. The red lighthouse has been the island's beacon for over a hundred years — a fine symbol for a new beginning.

## Pine forest near De Koog

Soft light between the trunks, the scent of resin, and an intimacy you will not find anywhere else on the island. Perfect for a small ceremony.

## Sheep barn in the polder

Authentically Texel, with the calm of the hinterland. A surprising place for anyone looking for something other than beach or dunes.
